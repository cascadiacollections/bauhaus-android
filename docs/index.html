02 / From collection to canvas

An old landscape.
A new way to see it.

The Bauhaus service starts with a CC0 landscape from the Metropolitan Museum of Art or the Art Institute of Chicago, then uses neural style transfer with a curated artistic reference.

  1. A public-domain starting point. Museum collections provide the original landscape.
  2. A different artistic lens. References include Monet, Hokusai, Cézanne, and Turner.
  3. A place in your day. The Android app fetches the result and lets you make it your wallpaper.

04 / Make room for art

Your next wallpaper
is waiting.

Bauhaus runs on Android 15 and newer. The FOSS edition has no Google or proprietary dependencies. Nightly builds are for early adopters and may contain bugs.

View releases ↗
  1. Download an installable APK. Choose bauhaus-android-nightly.apk from GitHub Releases. Nightlies are development builds; read the release notes before installing.
  2. Open Bauhaus. Install the APK, then fetch today's artwork using an internet connection.
  3. Choose its home. Set your home screen, lock screen, or both. Enable background updates for a daily refresh.
A few details

Good to know.

Is the artwork free to use?

The daily scheduled artwork is published as CC0. Each image includes its own license and upstream source in the service metadata and app. Check the individual artwork's attribution.

Will my wallpaper change automatically?

Enable background updates in the app. Android schedules that work, so the refresh time can vary with battery and background restrictions.

Can I keep an older wallpaper?

Yes. Browse the archive, favorite a work, or reapply a day you love. You can also save artwork to your gallery.

Is Bauhaus open source?

Yes. The Android app is MIT licensed. The artwork service is also available on GitHub. App licensing and artwork licensing are listed separately.
